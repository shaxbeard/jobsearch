                How we collect the data

                The job listing come from a two-stage process. First, we do a Google search for jobs on applicant tracking systems (ATS) that employers use to advertise jobs. Second, we retrieve the job details directly from those ATS companies to get a complete view of the posting.

                Compare the results here with Hiring Cafe, which scrapes job postings directly from a list of known company websites. Hiring Cafe finds jobs at established companies while HitListMagic Cafe finds jobs at smaller companies and startups that use the ATS platforms.
